Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 · No data is sold

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Teto Anti-Spam bot processes and why.

1. Who we are

Teto Anti-Spam is an independent Discord bot operated by its developers (not affiliated with Discord Inc.). Contact details are listed in the Contact section.

2. Data we process

Depending on usage and configuration, the Bot may process:

  • Discord snowflake IDs — user IDs, guild (server) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs.
  • Message metadata & content (transient) — message content and timing are processed in memory to detect spam patterns. Content is not retained as a long-term message archive.
  • Moderation state — warning counts per user per guild, and related short-lived locks.
  • Server settings you configure — blacklist role ID and “unharmed” role IDs.
  • Operational logs — limited technical logs used to keep the Bot running.

3. Purpose of processing

  • Detect and mitigate spam and disruptive messaging.
  • Apply configured moderation actions (warnings, timeouts, role assignment, message cleanup).
  • Honor server configuration (unharmed roles, blacklist role, ignored channels).
  • Operate owner-only utility commands when used by authorized owners.
  • Maintain reliability and diagnose failures.

4. Legal bases / nature of processing

Processing is performed to provide the moderation service requested when the Bot is invited to a server, based on legitimate interest in keeping communities usable and on the configuration choices of server administrators. Message content is processed only as needed for real-time spam detection and related moderation.

5. Data storage & retention

  • In-memory history used for spam detection is short-lived and discarded after detection windows expire.
  • Warning counts and server settings are stored so the Bot can function across restarts until reset, decay, or removal of the Bot / data purge.
  • We do not operate a public archive of user messages.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share user data with advertisers. Data may be visible to host/infrastructure providers strictly as needed to run the Bot, and may be disclosed if required by law or to protect safety and rights. Discord itself processes data according to Discord’s own Privacy Policy when you use Discord.

7. Third parties

  • Discord — the platform; the Bot uses the Discord API under Discord’s Developer Terms.
  • Hosting — the machine or service where the Bot process runs may process technical data required for operation.

8. Your choices & rights

  • Server admins can remove the Bot at any time, which stops further processing for that server.
  • Admins can adjust unharmed roles, blacklist role, and related settings via the Bot’s commands.
  • You may contact us to request information about data associated with your Discord ID in stored settings/warnings, or to request deletion where applicable.
  • You can also exercise rights available under your local law by contacting us with your Discord user ID.

9. Children

The Bot is intended for use in Discord servers in accordance with Discord’s age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from children in a manner incompatible with Discord’s policies.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored settings and operational data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please configure the Bot with least-privilege roles and review permissions regularly.

11. International users

The Bot may be hosted in a different region from where users are located. By using the Bot you understand that necessary operational data may be processed in the region where the Bot is hosted.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the Bot after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact

For privacy requests, data deletion related to stored warnings/settings, or questions about these documents, contact the bot operators via the support channel or owners listed for your deployment of Teto Anti-Spam, or open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository (if published).

Please include your Discord user ID and the server ID involved so requests can be handled accurately.
